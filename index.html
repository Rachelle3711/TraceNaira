Terms of Service

Last updated July 2026

1. About TraceNaira

TraceNaira is a transaction tracking tool that helps Nigerian bank users understand the status of their transfers. We provide intelligent estimates based on known patterns of Nigerian interbank settlement — not live bank data.

2. What We Do

TraceNaira helps you track pending, processing, and failed bank transfers. Our estimates are based on publicly known settlement timelines and community-reported outcomes. We do not have direct access to NIBSS, CBN, or any bank's internal systems.

3. What We Don't Do

TraceNaira does not move, hold, or process money. We are not a financial institution. We do not guarantee the accuracy of any estimate or prediction. Always contact your bank directly for official transaction disputes.

4. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy of transaction details you enter into TraceNaira. Incorrect details will produce incorrect estimates. Never share your bank PIN, password, or OTP with anyone including TraceNaira.

5. Estimates Are Not Guarantees

All time estimates provided by TraceNaira are based on historical patterns and are not guaranteed. Actual transaction times may vary due to bank systems, public holidays, weekends, and network issues.

6. Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of TraceNaira after changes means you accept the updated terms.

7. Contact

For questions about these terms contact us at support@tracenaira.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

1. What We Collect

TraceNaira collects only the information you provide directly:

  • Your name and email address when you sign up
  • Your phone number for verification
  • Transaction details you manually enter — amount, banks, time
  • Transaction status updates you report

2. What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or store:

  • Your bank account number or BVN
  • Your bank PIN or password
  • Any data directly from your bank
  • Your location
  • Any biometric data

3. How We Store Your Data

Currently all your data is stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. This means your data never leaves your device and is not stored on any external server.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to:

  • Personalize your experience — greeting you by name
  • Display your transaction history
  • Calculate accurate estimates for your transfers
  • Improve our estimation models over time

5. We Never Sell Your Data

TraceNaira does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes. Ever.

6. Your Rights

You can delete all your data at any time by clearing your browser storage or using the clear history feature in the app. You have full control of your data at all times.

7. Children

TraceNaira is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Contact

For privacy concerns contact us at privacy@tracenaira.com
